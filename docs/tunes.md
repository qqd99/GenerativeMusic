## Source-level license notes

- Artform's [Christmas score collection](https://jamescarterpiano.com/sheet-music/christmas/) states that every score in the collection is public domain and free to read, print and keep. Individual carol pages repeat that score-level statement.
- The Cantorion Jingle Bells link is identified as a first-edition score reference. The IMSLP Silent Night link is a public-domain score entry.
- Public-domain status can depend on jurisdiction and on the exact edition/arrangement. The repository records the source's claim; it does not provide legal advice or claim universal copyright clearance.
- No CC0 source is asserted. No modern arrangement, sound recording, image, or audio file has been copied.
- The arrangements rendered by this project (bass, string pads, bell responses, rhythmic parts, timbre and form) are generated algorithmically and are not copied from the linked piano arrangements.

## Required final audit

For each tune, compare every encoded pitch, onset and duration to the exact linked score edition, record the score edition/version and jurisdiction basis, then update the status from “Review pending” only after a human reviewer signs off. If a melody does not match the source, correct it or replace it with an auditable score transcription.
