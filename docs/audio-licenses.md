# Audio and license record

## Included audio

There are no third-party recordings, sample packs, sound effects, voices or artwork in the repository. All music instruments are synthesized at runtime using Web Audio oscillators, gain envelopes, filters, stereo panning and deterministic generated noise for the sleigh-bell texture. The decoded sample cache is zero bytes because no audio samples are decoded.

Gift/reward sound effects are deliberately out of scope and are not mixed into the background music engine. A future app integration can route SFX separately through the app-owned SFX bus.

## Composition sources

The 12 carol score links and source-level license statements are documented in [tunes.md](./tunes.md). Source pages identify the scores as public domain; individual melody transcriptions still require note-by-note review before they can be described as score-verified. No CC0 claim is made.

## Procedural arrangements

The generated bass, chord pads, melody timbres, bell responses, sleigh texture, dynamics, variations and transitions are original algorithmic arrangements. They are not transcriptions of the piano accompaniment or recordings on the source pages.

## Future owner-supplied samples

No samples are currently needed. If owner-supplied samples are added later, record creator, source, exact license, attribution text, jurisdiction/territory, file hash and the owner's proof of rights before committing them. Do not add downloaded artwork/audio or samples with unclear provenance.
