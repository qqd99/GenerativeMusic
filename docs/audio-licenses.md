# Audio and license record

No third-party recordings, sample packs, artwork, sound effects or voice recordings are included. Runtime instruments are generated procedurally using browser oscillator, gain, filter, panner and buffer-noise nodes. No asset downloads are performed.

Tune score references and audit status are recorded in tunes.md. Composition rights and score-edition rights are separate; only verified PD/CC0 score editions may be transcribed. Any future owner-supplied sample must include author, source, exact license, attribution requirement and file hash before use.
