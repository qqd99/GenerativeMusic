About the engine

Composition and scheduling are pure deterministic TypeScript modules. Playback accepts a host-supplied BaseAudioContext and output AudioNode. The app owns the audio context, master volume, mute and speech ducking. The music engine owns arrangement, voice synthesis, crossfades and repertoire rotation.

No downloaded samples are used. The synthesizer uses oscillators, envelopes, filters, stereo panning and deterministic generated noise for sleigh-bell texture. Decoded sample cache: 0 MB.
